Our mission

Built for the desk, not the classroom.

The Capital Markets Club gives Dhillon School of Business students the technical skills, industry access and recruiting preparation needed for careers in investment banking, sales and trading, equity research, asset management and corporate development.

We serve students on the Lethbridge and Calgary campuses, in person and online.

What we do

Four ways to build a career in the markets.

Analyst Program

A selective technical program in two streams: Investment Banking, and Sales and Trading.

Weekly Insights

A standing weekly session on the market's major headlines and what moved them.

Speaker Series

Virtual and in-person sessions with professionals who have worked on large transactions.

Competitions

Club teams compete externally, including the National Investment Banking Competition.
