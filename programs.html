Programs

The Analyst Program.

Two streams. One standard.

Stream one

Investment Banking

Investment banking, corporate finance and corporate development. Analysts build merger recommendations and fairness opinions, debate hypothetical transactions, and prepare for the National Investment Banking Competition.

Stream two

Sales and Trading

Sales and trading, equity research and asset management. Analysts develop market analysis skills, compete in stock pitch and trading competitions, and train in the Dhillon School of Business trading room.

How admission works

A published rubric, applied to every applicant.

  1. Apply by email
  2. Interview with two reviewers
  3. Written decision
  4. Begin the stream

Intake opens every fall and may reopen in winter. Each intake announcement publishes the seats per stream, the deadline, the scoring rubric and the expected time commitment.

Applicants normally hold a cumulative GPA of 3.70 or higher; the Executive may admit applicants below that threshold who show exceptional technical readiness. Unsuccessful applicants remain full members and may apply again.
