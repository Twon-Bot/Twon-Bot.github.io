About

About the Club.

A student-run educational and professional development club at the University of Lethbridge.

Mission

A thorough understanding of capital markets roles, and the skills to win them.

The Club exists to help members understand the roles within capital markets and to develop the practical skills and industry knowledge required for careers in investment banking, sales and trading, equity research, asset management and corporate development.

We pursue that through case competitions, technical workshops, stock pitch and financial modelling exercises, guest speaker sessions and peer mentorship. Programming is structured so that students at every level of knowledge can participate and progress.

Analysts in the Sales and Trading Stream train in the Dhillon School of Business trading room, building practical familiarity with securities markets and trading environments.

Governance

Five elected offices, one public constitution.

OfficeResponsible for
Club PresidentSenior officer of the Club; chairs the Executive and represents the Club.
President, Investment BankingLeads the Investment Banking Stream: curriculum, intake and completion records.
President, Sales and TradingLeads the Sales and Trading Stream: curriculum, intake and completion records.
VP, Operations & ExternalSocial media, external communications, the events calendar and this website.
VP, Finance & InternalThe Club bank account, financial records, the membership list and the Official Analyst Register.

Executives are elected annually by Voting Members. Our Constitution is public.

What we are not

The Club does not manage money, hold client assets or provide investment advice. All research, pitches and models produced by the Club and its members are prepared for educational purposes.
